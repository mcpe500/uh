## Research Purpose & Scope

This fork serves as the experimental harness for academic research and a thesis focusing on:
1. **Runtime & Execution Efficiency**: Investigating ultra-lightweight execution profiles using the **Bun** runtime and native SQLite storage.
2. **Context Window & Token Optimization**: Implementing middle-truncation pruning, cache-friendly conversation compaction, and empirical token accounting.
3. **Extensibility & Standards**: Evaluating on-demand Skill injection protocols, Model Context Protocol (MCP) integrations, and multi-modal reference attachments (`@file` and image encoding).
